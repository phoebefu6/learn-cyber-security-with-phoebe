Learn Cyber Security with Phoebe

Every breach of a data estate walks a path: from somewhere the attacker already is, through a credential that should not have worked, a bucket that should not have been public or a package that should not have run, to the one table worth the trip. This course teaches defence by drawing that path first. Ironwood, a mid-size retailer, starts with nine open routes to its customers table and a backup that goes with the admin login. Six leader sessions on the attacker's view, the six functions of NIST CSF 2.0, the ATT&CK kill chain through a data stack, why time travel is not a backup, detecting what you cannot prevent, and the first hour. Eight builder sessions that inventory one estate and close the doors one control at a time, ending on a bench that counts what remains and whether anybody would see it used.

What is real here 🧾
